Notes:
  • Templates are identical across scenarios (σ on the fine √s
    grid); only which points enter the fit changes.
  • The Azzurri-like upper point is OUR dσ/dΓ_W=0 crossing
    (snapped to the 0.1-GeV grid), not Azzurri's 162.3 GeV.
  • The Azzurri-like layout reduces ρ (here +0.19 vs +0.51 for
    the 7-point) — the upper point carries little Γ_W info, partly
    decorrelating the POIs — but NOT to ρ≈0: the corr-lumi-only
    constraint leaves a residual (Azzurri's ρ≈0 uses a different
    setup). It also gives the tightest σ_ΓW.
  • The 'free-lumi' σ is meaningless for the 2-point Azzurri-like
    scenario (huge values): 2 points cannot constrain 2 POIs PLUS
    a floating normalisation — read its 'prior' (realistic) column.
